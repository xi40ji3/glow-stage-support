Glow Stage privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Glow Stage is a learning game for young children. We built it so that nothing about your child ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Glow Stage has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. The app does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • The name you type for the hero.
  • The optional face photo you take or choose. It is stored only inside the app on this device and is never uploaded.
  • Game progress, stars, stickers, items and the parent report (which question types were answered right or wrong).

You can remove the photo at any time in Settings, clear the parent report in the Parent report page, or delete everything by deleting the app.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo access only when a parent or child taps the photo button, so a face can be put on the hero. The photo is processed on the device and never leaves it.

Parents

The Parent report is protected by a grown-up question. There are no purchases inside the app and no links to other websites.
