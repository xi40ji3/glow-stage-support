Glow Stage support

Glow Stage is a cute idol-guardian adventure where children aged 6 to 8 practise Primary 1 and 2 math and English to win back the music from the grumpy Hushes.

Help

  • No sound? Turn off silent mode and tap the sound button in Settings.
  • Change the level or subject: tap Settings on the main page.
  • See how your child is doing: tap Parents on the main page and answer the grown-up question.
  • Start over: delete the app and install it again. All progress is stored only on the device.

Contact

Email glowstage.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
